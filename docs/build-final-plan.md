# Final build plan: light, premium, 3D design

Plan for `docs/build-final-prompt.md`, with `docs/prototype/final-3d.html` as the design reference. The questions in section 11 were answered on 2026-11-01; the decisions are recorded in section 12 and override anything above that disagrees.

## 1. What I read and what I found

Read: `AGENTS.md` and the Next.js 16.4 guides on fonts, caching, and "Preventing flash before hydration" (its theme recipe is exactly what section 4.1 asks for: `data-theme="light"` on `<html>`, a tiny inline `<head>` script, `suppressHydrationWarning`); `README.md`; `docs/architecture.md`, `design.md`, `content.md`; ADRs 0002, 0008, 0009, 0011, 0012; `src/config/site.ts`; every file in `src/content/**`; `src/components/three/**`, `src/components/motion/**`; `scripts/guard.mjs`; and the prototype in full (tokens, both themes, every section and script).

Current state that shapes the plan:

- The last commit (`751e208`, work in progress) added a dark "motion system": GSAP with ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll, Motion, an isometric services stack, a haircut WhatsApp demo, a scroll-filled process timeline, a terminal CTA posting to `submitBrief`, and a live hero circuit. The final design replaces almost all of it.
- `submitBrief` is a stub from that commit: it validates and logs the brief, and does not deliver it to the lead pipeline (which needs a name, a message, Turnstile, and an email code). Section 5.14 asks to keep the terminal wired to it.
- Lenis contradicts ADR 0008, which the prompt keeps. GSAP and Motion are not needed by any primitive in the final design.
- `/start` has no `?service=<slug>` preselect yet. Because marketing pages are `ensureStatic = "navigation"`, it has to be read on the client (`useSearchParams` inside `Suspense`), not in the page.
- `simple-icons` (latest 16.36.0) has 56 of the 63 marks we need. Missing: AWS, Microsoft Azure, OpenAI, Twilio, Plivo, Ahrefs, Looker Studio (see section 7).
- Prototype contrast, computed for both themes (section 6.2): dark passes everywhere; light has five pairs under 4.5:1.

## 5. Theme, type, and shape

- `tokens.css` rewritten with the prototype's semantic tokens: light on `:root`, dark on `:root[data-theme="dark"]` and under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` for `:root:not([data-theme="light"])`; exposed to Tailwind 4 with `--color-*: initial` kept.
- Default light: the inline script applies a stored `light` or `dark`, otherwise sets `light`. The toggle is a header `<button>` with a sun or moon icon and an `aria-label` naming the theme it switches to; it stores the choice in `localStorage` inside try/catch and dispatches a `themechange` event that 3D panels listen for.
- Fonts with `next/font/google`, self-hosted: Bricolage Grotesque (600, 700, 800), Figtree (400 to 700), JetBrains Mono (400, 500). Archivo removed; a static Bricolage Grotesque TTF and its OFL licence in `src/assets/fonts` for Open Graph images.
- The `type-*` scale from section 4.2; radii pill, 22 to 26, 18, 12 px; dotted backgrounds on hero and process; `--bg-soft` bands.
- Admin forced dark in its layout; Open Graph images and emails moved to the light palette with solid colours.

### 6.2 Contrast fixes to the prototype's light values

Dark passes every pair. These light pairs fall under 4.5:1 for text; I propose the smallest change that passes, keeping the hue.

| Pair (light)                               | Prototype     | Proposed                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `--amber` text on white and on `--bg-soft` | 3.99 and 3.70 | Darken to pass 4.5 on `--bg-soft`; amber stays for live indicators only                |
| `--ok` text on `--bg-soft`                 | 4.29          | Darken slightly to pass on `--bg-soft`                                                 |
| `--c-ai` used as text                      | 4.30          | Category colours are used for icons and dots only (3:1 is enough there); never as text |
| WhatsApp button, white on `#1f9d55`        | 3.49          | Darker green that passes 4.5 with white                                                |
| Editor comments and gutter on `--ed-bg`    | 3.96 and 1.95 | Lighten both to pass 4.5                                                               |

Everything else in the prototype passes: body text is 7.5 to 8.1 in light and 7.7 to 9.0 in dark, and `--accent-ink` on `--accent` is 4.95 in light and 9.15 in dark.

## 8. Performance plan

Before (initial JavaScript, gzipped, production build of `751e208`): `/` 179.7 KB, `/services` 175.7, service pages 179.6, `/process` 174.5, `/about` 179.8, `/contact` 174.3, `/admin/login` 179.1. Mobile Lighthouse for `/` measured 78 to 89 on that build.

- Remove GSAP, Lenis, and Motion (question 2), which also removes their loaders from every page.
- Server-render every primitive's first frame (orbit chip positions, sphere chips, stepper, cards), so the page paints without JavaScript and hydration only attaches behaviour.
- Below the fold, load each interactive primitive with `LazyWhenNear` and a same-size placeholder: marquee behaviour, demos, editor, sphere, pricing interactions, FAQ.
- Every loop runs through one shared frame loop and stops off screen and in background tabs.
- Inner pages: WebGL never in the measured window (question 6), still SVG first, no WebGL probe during hydration.
- Report initial JavaScript per route and Lighthouse (median of three, both before and after) at the end of each phase.

## 9. Order of work

Each phase ends with `pnpm check`, `pnpm build`, a commit, Playwright screenshots of the changed pages in both themes at 1440 and 390 px, and a stop for your "continue".

1. Phase 0: ADRs 0013 and 0014, `design.md` rewrite (rules and contrast tables), guard and lint changes, `0001-versions.md`.
2. Phase 1: tokens and both themes, theme script and toggle, fonts, shape utilities, `TechLogo` and `tools.ts`, every primitive, the `themechange` hook for 3D, WhatsApp button, `/design/final-kit`.
3. Phase 2: home page in section order, with pricing, industries, FAQ, demos, logos, and the restyled header, footer, and CTA.
4. Phase 3: `ServicePageLayout` rebuilt and the seven service pages with their models and demos.
5. Phase 4: process, about, work, case study, contact, start (with `?service=`), legal, 404, error pages, admin theme lock, Open Graph images, emails.
6. Phase 5: tests (tokenizer, pricing `known()`, tools list, theme script, e2e for sections and the toggle, baselines for both themes), Lighthouse report, docs (`design.md`, `content.md`, `handoff.md`, README), and the final list of open `[OWNER: ...]` placeholders.

## 10. Risks

- Home at 90 on mobile Lighthouse with an animated hero is the tightest target; the plan above keeps the first paint static, but question 7 covers what to give up if it still falls short.
- Sixteen procedural models are the largest piece of work; they share one material set and a small kit of shapes to stay consistent and light.
- The theme toggle changes every surface at once, including the 3D panels, which re-read colours on `themechange`.
